## Route choices

- Coffee & Campus: Market → Seven Seeds → Old Quad → Baillieu. About 46 minutes including 25 minutes of pauses in this model.
- Gardens & Quiet Corners: Market → Lincoln Square → System Garden → Baillieu. Best during the garden’s weekday public hours.
- Architecture & Art: Market → City Baths exterior → Potter Museum → Baillieu. Choose during Potter’s gallery hours.

Grainger is intentionally optional because its public opening is restricted. The app does not label any venue “open now”. A walk from the market on a non-trading day still starts outside; browsing stalls would require a market trading day.

Local fallback and route data: OSM API map extract retrieved 14 October 2026 for bbox `144.953,-37.809,144.966,-37.795`. `map-data.js` contains geographic features, coordinates, and precomputed paths. It is an ODbL-derived database and is distributed under the Open Database Licence: https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/ . The API source is https://www.openstreetmap.org/api/0.6/map?bbox=144.953,-37.809,144.966,-37.795 .

Paths are shortest paths in a filtered OSM pedestrian network, excluding mapped private/no-access, prohibited-foot, indoor and non-ground-level ways. Venue pins snap to nearby connected outdoor paths (1–24 metres from the mapped venue point). They are not surveyed entrance positions. Walking estimates use 4.5 km/h plus suggested pause durations; queues, signals, temporary closures, exhibitions and weather are not live inputs. Step-free routing is not verified. Bike/transit options remain explicitly labelled timing demos using the displayed walking path, not mode-specific directions.

The local map fallback is genuine geographic OSM data styled locally, not an invented street diagram. It covers this small precinct only. No OSM raster tiles are bulk downloaded. OpenFreeMap standard maps require a network connection; the local snapshot remains underneath if the network is unavailable.
